# BlueShield

BlueShield is a modified, combined distribution of:

- **uBlock Origin** by Raymond Hill and contributors (Chromium MV3 "Lite" build)
- **Privacy Badger** by the Electronic Frontier Foundation and contributors

The components retain their respective copyrights, notices and licenses.
BlueShield is distributed under GNU GPL v3 or later. The full upstream licence
texts are included in the packaged extension under `licenses/`; they are kept
unmodified and are deliberately not shown in the user interface.

Privacy Badger incorporates additional third-party code. Its complete upstream
licence and attribution notices are available in the local `privacybadger-mv3/LICENSE`
source file and in the upstream source distribution.

BlueShield is an independent build and is not endorsed by the uBlock Origin or
Privacy Badger projects. Upstream promotional, donation, social-sharing and
error-reporting links were removed from the interface; no browsing data is sent
to either upstream project.
